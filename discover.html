GTA VI guide frequently asked questions

Where can I explore the GTA VI Leonida map?

Use the interactive Leonida map. It displays published poster artwork and lets you pan, zoom, search approximate grid references, and place your own waypoints. See the map sources page for attribution.

Are the map markers accurate in-game GPS coordinates?

No. Location markers currently refer to broad squares on the printed poster. They should not be treated as exact street-level coordinates, collectible positions or a working in-game GPS route.

Where can I find Vice City and other Leonida locations?

Explore the locations directory or search the interactive map. The printed poster provides a geographic reference, but exact mission and collectible locations require independent verification.

Can I track my GTA VI missions?

Yes. The mission index saves completion status in your browser. Its legacy 95-entry tracker is not a verified complete canonical mission sequence. The story catalog provides a separate chronological reference.

Can I track collectibles and discoveries?

The collectible journal supports found-status tracking and custom discoveries. Published leads should not be confused with confirmed GPS pins.

Will my progress transfer to another phone or computer?

Not automatically. Progress is stored in your browser. Use the Progress Vault to export a backup and restore it on another device. Clearing browser data can remove locally stored progress.

Is UGN an official Rockstar Games website?

No. UGN GTA VI Guide is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

How to help others find this guide

Share a direct link to the specific page that answers someone's question. For example, link to the Leonida map when discussing the map, or to the collectible journal when discussing collectibles. Share where community rules allow, and avoid unsolicited repeated posting.
