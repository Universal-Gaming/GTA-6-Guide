Publishing standards

  1. Officially confirmed: Supported directly by Rockstar or in-game material documented with clear context.
  2. Source-backed: Described by a named guide with a link to the relevant page; verify independently before assigning exact map coordinates.
  3. Personal discovery: Created by a player, stored locally, never presented as verified game content.
  4. Unverified or pending: Leave details blank or clearly labeled until evidence is available. Never invent mission rewards, collectible counts, exact coordinates or unlock conditions.
  5. Spoiler protection: Keep major story reveals behind explicit player controls and warn before point-of-no-return missions.

How to submit a correction

Include the page URL, exact incorrect statement, replacement text, the source URL, and—if possible—the platform and game version. For locations, provide a clearly described in-game reference point. Do not upload proprietary game artwork or map tiles without permission.
