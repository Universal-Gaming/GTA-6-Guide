Pool Day

The mission between Another Life and Company Cars. This standalone guide keeps existing saved mission IDs and links intact while the mission catalog is being reconciled.

Step-by-step walkthrough

  1. After Another Life, return to Jason at the Y Hotel and speak with Lucia on the balcony. Agree to join her at the pool.
  2. Once seated at the cabana, walk past the pool to the bar on the right and order Lucia a Tequila Sunrise.
  3. On the way back, Eddie Acosta approaches and invites you to a party. His invitation creates an optional follow-up mission; accepting or declining does not prevent the invitation.
  4. Return to the cabana and give Lucia her cocktail.
  5. During the conversation, look at the man floating in the flamingo, the men to the left and the woman with the large hat on the pool edge as prompted.
  6. When Lucia gets up, follow her out to the beach. Valentina calls afterward, unlocking the next story objectives.

Unlocks: Company Cars and Lawyer Up become available after Pool Day. Eddie Acosta's optional party invitation also begins here.
