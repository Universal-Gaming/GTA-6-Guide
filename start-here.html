How to use this guide

  1. Start with the Field Guide. Browse the region and character profiles. Search by name or category.
  2. Open the Map Explorer. Drag, zoom and save regions you want to revisit. Current marker positions are schematic, not exact in-game coordinates.
  3. Track your discoveries. Saved guide entries and map selections are stored locally in this browser. They are not synchronized between devices.
  4. Verify detailed walkthroughs. Follow primary-source links and look for clear verification notes before relying on any mission or collectible instructions.

Accuracy and spoiler policy

What this guide does not claim: Our current map is an illustrative prototype, not Rockstar's exact map. We do not yet have a tested, complete mission-by-mission walkthrough, all collectible coordinates or verified 100% completion checklist. These will be published only when researched and tested.

We separate confirmed Rockstar information from independent research, and we avoid placing unverified gameplay details into articles as fact. Original graphics are used instead of redistributing copyrighted game artwork.
