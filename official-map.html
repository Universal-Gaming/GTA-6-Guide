VERIFIED POSTER REFERENCE

Leonida's five counties and major places

The official printable poster names five counties. The map has a printed A–M / 1–9 grid; the squares below are broad references, not exact street addresses or gameplay coordinates.

Lummox County

Northern Leonida: Mount Kalaga National Park (C5), Bonanza (B5), Grayson (C4) and Millport (C6–C7).

Leonard County

Central and northeastern Leonida: Lake Leonida (E6), Ambrosia (E5–E6), Waning Sands (E7) and Belville (F6–F7).

Kelly County

Western Leonida: Port Gellhorn (E3), Draper Island (D2–D3) and Capri (G3).

Vice-Dale County

Vice City (G7), Vice Beach (G8) and Vice City International Airport (H5–H6).

Mariana County

Southern wetlands and islands: Grassrivers (I4–I5), Hamlet (I5–I6), Watson Bay (J4), Key Lento (K6), Southwind (L3) and Mile Zero Air Station (L3).

Roads and navigation

The poster distinguishes interstates, U.S. routes, state routes, county routes and toll roads. Its printed scale is a poster reference, not verified in-game travel distance. The map does not include mission, collectible or GPS route markers.

How UGN verifies locations

We record a location name, its source, the type of evidence, and whether its coordinates have been independently validated. Until coordinates are verified, locations remain unpinned or are clearly identified as schematic. Exact collectible routes, mission steps and GPS navigation will not be presented as confirmed without testing.
