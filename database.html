A searchable collection of GTA VI places, cast, named vehicles and customization items documented in Rockstar's published materials. Each entry links to official media for its images and evidence.

Evidence standard: These are names appearing in official Rockstar profiles and media labels. A screenshot label does not establish vehicle performance, spawn locations, prices, ownership or availability in every game edition. We link to official images rather than copying or hotlinking game assets.
Independent fan project, not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Entries are a verified-source index, not a complete gameplay encyclopedia.
